# Markie RS polish pass, 2026-11-25

Branch `t3/markie-rs-agents-setting-polish`. Kirby: "Let's polish the UI even further." He also asked for document pages with a max width and a created date.

## How it was checked

Every main surface was drawn off screen with the scene harness ([SCREEN.md](SCREEN.md)), in light and dark. Each was drawn at 1280 × 820 (the default), at 760 pt (narrow), and for the document pages at 1900 pt (wide).

- Surfaces: Recent (grid and list), Browse, Cloud (signed in and signed out), Settings (Account, Appearance, Files, Agents, Advanced), a document in rich view and in Source view, the History page, the Share dialog, the command palette, the shortcuts sheet and the welcome page.
- Left out: the Projects tab and the Vault page. Other agents are rebuilding them.
- Before: the branch with Part A (Settings › Agents) and no polish. After: the branch head. The rail before Part A comes from the untouched `rust/markie-rs` build.
- All documents and accounts are made up (`seed-home.mjs`, `seed.mjs`, `browse-fixture.py`).
- Electron's design was read from its source, `src/app/globals.css` and the components. The headless Electron harness was not run in this pass.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rows and cards stretch edge to edge on a wide window. Names sit far from their dates and badges. | Recent, Cloud, Browse | Fixed. One centred column, at most 1200 pt with its padding. | 9 | At 760 pt the filter placeholders clip: "Filter by name or fol…" on Recent, "Find a document by r…" on Browse. | Recent, Browse | Left. The field shrinks so the controls keep one row. A shorter placeholder would change Electron's wording. |
